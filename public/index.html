      How does the free trial work?

      Pick a plan and connect Webflow. Polar, our payment provider, asks for a card but charges nothing for 7 days, and every feature of the plan is open from the first minute. Cancel any time in those 7 days from your Account page and you pay nothing; otherwise the plan starts on day 8. Polar emails you a reminder before the trial ends. One free trial per customer, across the products of Silit Labs.

      How is this different from an SEO tool?

      SEO tools track where your pages rank in Google's list of links. AI SEO Deck tracks whether AI answers name you and link to you, keyword by keyword and engine by engine, and helps you fix the page that should win. Many teams use both.

      Why keywords and not prompts?

      Keywords are how you already plan SEO and content, and they are what the AI searches for before it answers. Each keyword is checked with the questions people really ask, so you get a steady score instead of one lucky or unlucky answer. You can read and edit those questions at any time.

      Which AI engines do you check?

      ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude and Grok through their official APIs with live web search on, and Google AI Overviews and AI Mode from live Google results. The free plan covers ChatGPT, Google AI Overviews and Gemini; paid plans cover all six.

      How do you know what the AI says?

      We ask each engine the way a person would and read the full answer and its sources. Answers vary between people and days, so scores are built from many answers and followed over weeks rather than taken from a single reply.

      Where do keyword ideas and search volumes come from?

      Search volumes, keyword ideas and the keywords where Google's AI Overviews cite your site come from DataForSEO, a search data provider. Related searches also come from the AI engines themselves, from the web searches they run while answering.

      Does it change my site without asking?

      No. Every fix is shown first and written to Webflow only when you press Apply. Changes arrive as unpublished edits and CMS drafts, so nothing goes live until you publish in Webflow.

      Which Webflow plans does it work with?

      No particular plan. AI SEO Deck works through the Webflow API on any site, also one without a paid site plan. Titles and descriptions are written to static pages. Answers and new pages go to your CMS as drafts, which needs a site with the Webflow CMS. For Collection pages, Site health checks the SEO settings of the template.

      Will it slow down my site?

      No. AI SEO Deck adds no code, script or widget to your site. What you apply is a page title, a meta description or CMS drafts, and they wait in Webflow until you publish.

      Can I cancel any time?

      Yes, during the trial and after it. Paid plans run through Polar and can be cancelled from your Account page; a cancelled plan stays on until the end of the period you paid for. Deleting your account deletes everything we stored. To remove the app from Webflow, uninstall it in Webflow.
