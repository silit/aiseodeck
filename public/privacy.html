Privacy policy

Last updated: 22 November 2026. AI SEO Deck is run by Silit Labs.

What AI SEO Deck does

It checks the keywords you choose with AI assistants, reads your public website for an audit, and, when you ask it to, writes fixes to your Webflow site. This policy explains what it stores and why.

Webflow access

When you connect, Webflow asks which sites to share. The access token Webflow issues is stored encrypted so checks can run while you are away. It is never sent to your browser, logged or shared. Disconnecting revokes it at Webflow and deletes it. If you uninstall the app in Webflow, we delete the token and that connection's sites after the next refused request.

What we store

  • Your Webflow user id, name, email and the settings you choose (report email, agency name), and how many checks you used this month.
  • Per site: name, domains, your brand details, rivals and keywords, with the questions asked for each keyword and their monthly searches; the answers from AI assistants (an excerpt of up to 1,500 characters per answer, with the cited source links) for your latest checks, and summary numbers for up to 52 checks; the latest site audit (page titles, descriptions, detected questions and findings); keyword ideas and keywords cited in AI Overviews.
  • Suggestions you have not applied yet and related keyword lookups, for 14 days. Answers from checks of a single keyword are kept for up to 90 days.
  • For paid plans: plan, subscription status, renewal date and the Polar customer and subscription IDs. Card details go directly to Polar.

We do not read or store your form submissions, visitors or analytics.

AI providers and keyword data

Your keywords and the questions written for them are sent through their APIs to OpenAI (ChatGPT), Google (Gemini), Anthropic (Claude) and xAI (Grok) to get answers, and to DataForSEO to read Google AI Overviews and AI Mode for your country and language. To find keyword ideas, monthly searches, related searches and the keywords where Google's AI Overviews cite your site, your keywords and your site's domain are sent to DataForSEO. To write questions, ideas and suggestions, and to describe how AI talks about you, your keywords, text from your public pages and excerpts of answers that name your brand are sent to one of these AI providers.

AI Visibility Report

The report at /ai-visibility-report works without an account. When you check a website we store the address you typed and what its public home page says, the business name, country, searches and questions used for the check, the answers of the AI assistants with their source links, and the findings and fixes. For a paid report we also store the Polar order and checkout IDs and your email address, which we use only to send you the link and the re-check. To limit free checks we keep a keyed hash of your IP address with the check, never the address itself.

The home page text, the searches and the questions are sent to the AI providers and to DataForSEO in the way described above, and the public pages the assistants cite are opened to read who is named on them. An unpaid check is deleted after 30 days and a paid report after 400 days. To have one deleted sooner, write to us with its address. The page of a check is open to anyone who has its address and is marked not to be indexed by search engines. The done boxes of the fix kit are saved in your own browser only, and the report pages set no cookies. Their typefaces are loaded from Google Fonts.

Client report links

A report link is public to anyone who has it and is marked not to be indexed by search engines. Replace the link at any time to invalidate the old one.

Cookies

AI SEO Deck sets only the cookies it needs to work: one that keeps you signed in, and short-lived ones while you connect Webflow. There are no tracking, advertising or analytics cookies, and the site has no analytics.

Other services we use

  • Webflow (your sites and the API)
  • Vercel (hosting) and Upstash (database)
  • Resend (email)
  • Polar (payments)
  • OpenAI, Google, Anthropic and xAI (AI answers and suggestions)
  • DataForSEO (Google AI Overviews and AI Mode results, keyword data)

Our hosting provider keeps standard server logs for a limited time.

Deleting your data

Account > Delete account revokes every Webflow authorization and removes your account and all stored data immediately. Changes you already applied to your Webflow sites stay.
