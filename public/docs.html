How AI SEO Deck works

AI SEO Deck checks, keyword by keyword, whether AI assistants name and cite your brand, and helps you fix your Webflow site so they do it more often.

1. Connect Webflow

Press Connect Webflow and choose the sites or workspace to share. AI SEO Deck asks for these permissions:

PermissionWhy
authorized_user:readYour name and email, to create your account and send reports.
sites:readSite names and domains, so we know which website belongs to your brand.
pages:readPage titles, descriptions and existing structured data, to match keywords to pages and for the audit.
pages:writeOnly when you press Apply: saves a new title and description, or JSON-LD structured data, to that page.
cms:readLists your CMS collections, so you can pick where drafts go.
cms:writeOnly when you press Apply or Add the draft to Webflow: creates draft items. Drafts are never published by us.

Nothing is ever published for you. Every change arrives in Webflow as an unpublished edit or a CMS draft; you publish when you are ready.

On sites with several languages (Webflow Localization), fixes are written to the primary language only. The other languages keep their own text, and the app says so next to every Apply button.

2. Set up your brand and keywords

Enter your brand name as people say it, other names, your domains, your market and up to five rivals. Then pick the keywords to track. We suggest ideas written from your homepage, and the keywords where Google's AI Overviews already cite your site, each with its monthly searches.

3. How a keyword is checked

People ask AI assistants long questions, while the assistants search the web with short keywords before they answer. So every keyword is checked both ways:

  • ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude and Grok get two questions a customer would ask about the keyword, written in your language, through their official APIs with live web search on. You can read and edit the questions on the keyword's page.
  • Google AI Overviews and AI Mode get the keyword itself, from live Google results for your country and language.

On paid plans that is 10 AI answers per keyword, every week. The free plan checks each keyword monthly on ChatGPT, Google AI Overviews and Gemini (5 answers). For every answer we record whether your brand is named, its place among the brands you track, whether your site is cited as a source, which rivals are named, which websites are cited and what the AI searched for.

Visible is the share of answers that name you. AI answers vary between runs and people, so read it as a trend across weeks rather than a promise about a single answer.

Checks on demand

  • A keyword you add is checked right away, up to your plan's number of keywords in new keywords each month. After that, new keywords wait for the next scheduled check.
  • On paid plans you can check a keyword again from its page: 10 checks a month on Solo, 30 on Studio and 100 on Agency. Check all keywords now uses one check per keyword.

Fix next

Above your keywords, Fix next lists up to three keywords to work on first: the ones where the fewest engines name you, weighted by monthly searches and by rivals named in your place. Keywords you applied fixes to in the last two weeks move down until you check them again.

4. The keyword page

  • The answer: yes, sometimes or no, how many engines name you, your visibility over time and the rivals named instead.
  • What each engine said: every answer, with your brand marked, the sources it cited and the searches it ran.
  • What to do: the page that should rank for the keyword (matched from your pages, or chosen by you), a new title and description, answers to the questions behind the keyword as FAQ schema on the page and as CMS drafts, and the sites the AI relies on. If no page fits, we draft a new page from what your site already says and add it to your CMS as a draft.
  • Related keywords: the searches the AI ran and Google's related searches, with monthly searches. Track any of them in one click.

5. Discover

The keywords where Google's AI Overviews cite your site, keyword ideas for your site and the searches the AI ran for your keywords. Only the keywords you track count toward your plan.

6. Rivals and sources

  • Where rivals win: the keywords where an AI names a rival and not you, widest gaps first.
  • How AI describes you: whether the answers that name you sound positive, neutral or negative, the words they use for you, and claims worth checking, such as an old price.
  • Websites the AI relies on and your pages the AI cites, counted across all your keywords.

7. Site audit

The audit reads your published pages, robots.txt, llms.txt and sitemap.xml from your live domain and checks: titles and descriptions, H1 headings, JSON-LD structured data, questions answered without FAQ schema, social preview tags, thin content, image alt text, and AI crawlers blocked in robots.txt. It also matches each keyword to its best page.

The audit identifies itself with the user agent AISEODeck/1.0 (+https://aiseodeck.silitlabs.com/docs#bot) and only reads the domains of the sites you connected.

  • Page fixes: titles and descriptions, Organization and WebSite schema for the homepage (from the business details in Settings) and FAQPage schema. Existing schema on the page is kept; only the same types are replaced.
  • robots.txt and llms.txt: we show the lines to allow blocked AI search crawlers and give you an llms.txt to upload in Site settings > SEO > LLMs.txt.

Applying fixes to Webflow is part of the paid plans. On the free plan you can copy every suggestion.

Keyword data

Monthly searches, related searches and the keywords where Google's AI Overviews cite your site come from DataForSEO. Searches the AI ran come from the AI engines themselves.

Reports

After every scheduled check you get an email with your visibility and your keywords. Studio and Agency plans can share a read-only client report link with their agency name on it.

Plans and the free trial

Every paid plan starts with 7 days free. Pick a plan on the pricing page or in Settings and connect Webflow; Polar, our payment provider, takes a card without charging it, and everything in the plan is open right away. Cancel from Settings > Plan > Manage billing before the trial ends and nothing is charged; otherwise the plan starts on day 8. Polar emails you a reminder before the trial ends. One trial per customer. The free plan needs no card.

Disconnect and delete

Settings > Webflow connections > Disconnect revokes access and deletes the sites of that connection from AI SEO Deck. Settings > Delete account removes everything we stored. Changes you already applied to Webflow stay on your site.
