No login credentials are needed. Reviewers install the app with their own Webflow account from the Install URL, https://aiseodeck.silitlabs.com/auth. The AI SEO Deck account is made from that Webflow account at that moment; there is no separate username or password.

Highest tier: an account whose Webflow email ends in @webflow.com (marketplaceteam@webflow.com included) is on the Agency plan, the highest tier, from the moment it is made, automatically, and stays on it for as long as the account exists. Every feature is open.

For a reviewing account on another email address: open Account and, under Plan, enter this access code in the field "Access code": ACCESS_CODE_HERE
It opens the same Agency access on that account, and it stays.

Coming back later: "Sign in" on https://aiseodeck.silitlabs.com sends a one-time link to the email of the Webflow account.
