Questions

Which AI engines do you check?

ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, Claude and Grok through their official APIs with live web search on, and Google AI Overviews and AI Mode from live Google results. The free plan covers ChatGPT, Google AI Overviews and Perplexity; paid plans cover all seven.

How do you know what the AI says?

We ask each engine your questions the way a person would and read the full answer and its sources. Answers vary between people and days, so the grid shows trends across many questions and weeks rather than a single answer.

Does it change my site without asking?

No. Every fix is shown first and written to Webflow only when you press Apply. Changes arrive as unpublished edits, so nothing goes live until you publish in Webflow.

Which Webflow plans does it work with?

It works with sites on any Webflow plan. Fixes are written to static pages; for CMS pages you get the advice to apply in the collection template.

What does a question look like?

The way your customers would type it, for example "best dentist for veneers in Antalya" or "Ledgerbird alternatives". We suggest a first set from your homepage, in your language.

Can I cancel any time?

Yes. Paid plans run through Polar and can be cancelled from Settings. Deleting your account removes all stored data and revokes Webflow access.
