Privacy policy

Last updated: 13 November 2026. AI SEO Deck is run by Silit Labs.

What AI SEO Deck does

It sends the questions you choose to AI assistants, reads your public website for an audit, and, when you ask it to, writes fixes to your Webflow site. This policy explains what it stores and why.

Webflow access

When you connect, Webflow asks which sites to share. The access token Webflow issues is stored encrypted so checks can run while you are away. It is never sent to your browser, logged or shared. Disconnecting revokes it at Webflow and deletes it. If you uninstall the app in Webflow, we delete the token and that connection's sites after the next refused request.

What we store

  • Your Webflow user id, name, email and the settings you choose (report email, agency name).
  • Per site: name, domains, your brand details, rivals and questions; the answers from AI assistants (an excerpt of up to 1,500 characters per answer, with the cited source links) for your latest eight checks, and summary numbers for up to 52 checks; the latest site audit (page titles, descriptions, detected questions and findings).
  • Suggestions you have not applied yet, for 14 days.
  • For paid plans: plan, subscription status, renewal date and the Polar customer and subscription IDs. Card details go directly to Polar.

We do not read or store your form submissions, visitors or analytics.

AI providers

Your questions are sent through their APIs to OpenAI (ChatGPT), Google (Gemini), Perplexity, Anthropic (Claude) and xAI (Grok) to get answers, and to DataForSEO to read Google AI Overviews and AI Mode for your country and language. To describe how AI talks about you, excerpts of the answers that name your brand are sent to one of these AI providers. When you ask for a suggestion, the text of that public page is sent to one of them to write it. We do not send your email or account details to them.

Client report links

A report link is public to anyone who has it and is marked not to be indexed by search engines. Replace the link at any time to invalidate the old one.

Other services we use

  • Webflow (your sites and the API)
  • Vercel (hosting) and Upstash (database)
  • Resend (email)
  • Polar (payments)
  • cron-job.org (schedules the checks)
  • OpenAI, Google, Perplexity, Anthropic and xAI (AI answers and suggestions)
  • DataForSEO (Google AI Overviews and AI Mode results)

Our hosting provider keeps standard server logs for a limited time.

Deleting your data

Settings > Delete account revokes every Webflow authorization and removes your account and all stored data immediately. Changes you already applied to your Webflow sites stay.
