AI SEO Deck guide

Setup, use and removal on Webflow. AI SEO Deck checks, keyword by keyword, whether AI assistants name and cite your brand, and helps you improve the pages of your Webflow site so they do it more often. This guide covers the whole app: the web app at aiseodeck.silitlabs.com and the panel inside the Webflow Designer.

What you need before you start

  • A Webflow site, and the right to authorize apps for it. You authorize AI SEO Deck for single sites or for a whole Workspace on Webflow's own screen.
  • No particular Webflow plan. AI SEO Deck works through the Webflow API, which is open to every site, so no Workspace plan or site plan is required and a site without a paid site plan works too. Two features depend on what the site has: drafts in the CMS need a site with the Webflow CMS and a collection that has a Rich text or Plain text field, and the panel in the Designer needs access to the Designer of the site.
  • Nothing to install on your site. AI SEO Deck adds no script, no code and no element to your pages.
  • No other account. Your AI SEO Deck account is made from the Webflow authorization, with the email of your Webflow account. A card is asked only for a paid plan, by Polar, our payment provider.

Setup asks for: your brand name as people say it, other names it goes by (optional), the country your customers are in, the language of your keywords, up to five rivals by name (optional) and the keywords to track. It does not ask for a website address: the addresses of your site come from Webflow.

1. Install and authorize

Install AI SEO Deck from the Webflow Marketplace, or press Connect Webflow on aiseodeck.silitlabs.com. Both open Webflow's authorization screen, where you choose the sites or the Workspace to authorize. If you approve, your account is made and you land on the setup of your site, or on your Sites page when you authorized several. If you cancel, AI SEO Deck gets no access and stores nothing.

AI SEO Deck asks for these permissions and uses each one only as described:

PermissionWhat it is used for
authorized_user:readYour Webflow user id, name and email, to make your account and send your reports. Also needed to confirm who opens the panel in the Designer.
sites:readThe names, addresses (custom domains and the webflow.io address) and languages of the sites you authorized.
pages:readThe page list with each page's SEO and Open Graph settings, and the text, headings and images of static pages, for Site health and for writing suggestions from your own pages.
pages:writeOnly when you press Apply: saves a new SEO title and meta description to that one page.
cms:readLists your CMS collections and their fields, so you can choose where drafts go.
cms:writeOnly when you press Apply or Add the draft to Webflow: creates draft items in the collection you chose.

AI SEO Deck never publishes your site. Every change arrives in Webflow as an unpublished change or a CMS draft, and you publish in Webflow when you are ready.

2. Set up a site

  1. Your brand. The name we look for in AI answers, other names, your market and the language of your keywords. The page shows the addresses of your site as read from Webflow, with the time they were read and a Refresh from Webflow button. An answer that links to one of these addresses counts as a citation.
  2. Your rivals. Up to five businesses you compete with, by name. We show where AI names them instead of you.
  3. Your keywords. Type your own, or press Suggest keywords from my site. That button reads your home page through Webflow and sends its title, description and text to our AI provider for ideas; it also lists the keywords where Google's AI Overviews already cite your site. Monthly searches are Google data from DataForSEO.

Saving the setup starts the first check. You can change everything later in the site's Settings.

3. How a keyword is checked

People ask AI assistants long questions, while the assistants search the web with short keywords before they answer. So every keyword is checked both ways:

  • ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude and Grok get two questions a customer would ask about the keyword, written in your language, through their official APIs with live web search on. You can read and edit the questions on the keyword's page.
  • Google AI Overviews and AI Mode get the keyword itself, from live Google results for your country and language.

On paid plans that is 10 AI answers per keyword, every week. The free plan checks each keyword once a month on ChatGPT, Google AI Overviews and Gemini (5 answers). For every answer we record whether your brand is named, its place among the brands you track, whether your site is cited as a source, which rivals are named, which websites are cited and what the AI searched for.

Visible is the share of answers that name you. AI answers vary between runs and people, so read it as a trend across weeks, not as a promise about a single answer.

A check asks the AI engines and Google. It reads nothing from your site and writes nothing to Webflow. Before a scheduled check starts, AI SEO Deck asks Webflow one thing: whether the app is still authorized.

Checks on demand

  • A keyword you add is checked right away, up to your plan's number of keywords in new keywords each month. After that, new keywords wait for the next scheduled check.
  • On paid plans you can check a keyword again from its page: 10 checks a month on Solo, 30 on Studio and 100 on Agency. Check all keywords now uses one check per keyword.

4. The keyword page and its fixes

  • The answer: yes, sometimes or no, how many engines name you, your visibility over time and the rivals named instead.
  • What each engine said: every answer, with your brand marked, the sources it cited and the searches it ran.
  • Your fix plan: the page that should win the keyword (matched from your pages by Site health, or chosen by you), a new title and description for it, answers to the questions behind the keyword, and the websites the AI relies on for this keyword.
  • Related keywords: the searches the AI ran and Google's related searches, with monthly searches. Track any of them in one click.

Title and description

Press Write the fixes. AI SEO Deck reads the page from Webflow at that moment and an AI provider writes a title and a description from it. The plan then shows two sides: Now in Webflow, with the time the values were read, and Suggested, which you can edit. Nothing is written until you press Apply to Webflow. Just before writing, the app checks that the page in Webflow still has the values you were shown. If someone changed the page in the meantime, nothing is written and the new values are shown.

Answers as CMS drafts

The answers are written from your own page for the questions behind the keyword. You can copy them into the page in Webflow, or tick Add them to a collection: each answer becomes a draft item there. Drafts are never published by AI SEO Deck.

A page for a keyword that has none

If no page fits a keyword, choose A new page in the CMS. AI SEO Deck drafts a title, an intro and answers from what your site already says, and adds it to the collection you choose as one draft item.

Applying to Webflow is part of the paid plans. On the free plan you can read and copy every suggestion.

5. Discover

The keywords where Google's AI Overviews cite your site, keyword ideas for your site and the searches the AI ran for your keywords. Only the keywords you track count toward your plan.

6. Rivals and sources

  • Where rivals win: the keywords where an AI names a rival and not you, widest gaps first.
  • How AI describes you: whether the answers that name you sound positive, neutral or negative, the words they use for you, and claims worth checking, such as an old price.
  • Websites the AI relies on and your pages the AI cites, counted across all your keywords.

7. Site health

Site health reads your pages through the Webflow API and nothing else. It does not request your published website. Press Read my pages from Webflow to start; it runs only when you press the button.

What it reads: the page list with each page's SEO title, meta description and Open Graph title and description, and, for static pages, the text, headings and images as Webflow holds them. What it reports for each page:

  • no SEO title, or a title shorter than 20 or longer than 65 characters;
  • no meta description, or one shorter than 60 or longer than 165 characters;
  • no Open Graph title or description (one that copies the SEO value counts as set);
  • no H1 heading, or more than one;
  • less than 300 words of text;
  • images without alt text (an image marked decorative, or one that uses the alt text of its asset, is not a finding);
  • the questions the page answers, and the best page for each of your keywords.

What it does not report, because Webflow's API does not provide it: structured data, robots.txt, llms.txt and the sitemap. Three more limits, shown where they apply:

  • Collection pages. For a Collection template, the SEO settings of the template are checked. Its text comes from CMS items and is not read.
  • Components. Text inside components is not read. On a page that uses components, or that is too long to read in full, "No H1" and "Thin content" are shown as not determined and take nothing off the page's score.
  • Drafts and utility pages, such as the 404, password and search pages, are left out.

The screen says when the pages were read from Webflow and has a Refresh from Webflow button. Webflow limits how many API calls a site takes each minute, so a large site is read in parts: the screen shows which pages are not read yet and a Read the rest button. Site health covers 20 pages on Free, 100 on Solo, 150 on Studio and 250 on Agency.

Open a static page with Fix to see what it has in Webflow now, write a suggestion with AI, and apply a title and description or add answers to your CMS as drafts.

8. Inside the Webflow Designer

AI SEO Deck has a panel in the Designer. Open the Apps panel in the Designer and launch AI SEO Deck. The panel follows the page that is open:

  • This page: the keyword tracked for it, with a mark for each AI engine: names you, links your site, does not name you, no AI answer, not checked yet.
  • Fix this page: the title and description findings, made from the values the Designer has at that moment, and the headings, text and image findings from the last Site health read, with the time of that read.
  • Title and description: an editor that shows what the page has now next to the fields. Write with AI reads the page from Webflow and fills the fields with a suggestion, labelled Suggested by AI until you edit it. Apply to this page sets the two values on the open page through the Designer. Nothing is published.

The panel changes nothing until you press Apply, uses no keyboard shortcuts and stores nothing in your browser. When you move to another page in the Designer, the panel follows; an Apply you pressed finishes on the page you pressed it for. To know who is using it, it sends our server a short-lived Webflow ID token with the id of the site and of the page, and nothing else that identifies you. A site that is not connected yet gets a Connect button that opens your Sites page. On the free plan the panel shows everything and writes suggestions, and Apply is off.

The panel is built with Webflow's own colors and type for apps, works with the keyboard alone and names every control and every mark for screen readers.

10. Sites with several languages

On a site with Webflow Localization, AI SEO Deck reads and checks the primary language only, and suggestions are written from the pages in that language.

In the web app, titles, descriptions and CMS drafts are written to the primary locale, and the app says so next to every Apply button on such a site. Nothing is written to the other languages: what you localized there stays, and what a language inherits from the primary one follows it, as always in Webflow.

In the Designer panel, Apply to this page sets the title and description on the page as the Designer shows it at that moment, the same as typing them into the page settings there. The panel cannot see which language the Designer is showing. So on a site with more than one language the editor says this before you apply, names the language the suggestion was written from, and asks you to check which language is open.

11. Reports

After every scheduled check you get an email with your visibility and your keywords; you can turn it off on your Account page. On every plan you can create a read-only report link for a client, in the Settings of the site. On the Studio and Agency plans the report shows your agency name. Anyone who has the link can open it, it is marked not to be indexed by search engines, and you can replace it at any time, which ends the old link.

13. Disconnect, uninstall and delete

Disconnect a site

In the site's Settings, Disconnect this site deletes its keywords, answers and Site health results from AI SEO Deck. Nothing changes in Webflow and the app stays installed there. To connect the site again, open your Sites page and press Add a site: the sites you authorized are listed, read from Webflow, and Connect needs no new authorization. On your Account page, Disconnect does the same for all sites of one authorization.

Uninstall in Webflow

Access to Webflow is removed in Webflow, not in AI SEO Deck:

  • if you authorized single sites: Site settings, Apps & integrations, then Revoke next to AI SEO Deck;
  • if you authorized a whole Workspace: Workspace settings, Apps & integrations, then Uninstall app;
  • in the Designer: the Apps panel, AI SEO Deck, then Remove app.

After that, Webflow refuses AI SEO Deck's access token. AI SEO Deck notices at its next contact with Webflow, at the latest within a day, asks Webflow nothing more, and deletes the token and the sites of that authorization with everything kept about them. Your Sites page says what happened. To start again, install the app again.

If that was your last authorization and you have no paid plan, what is left is your account itself: your Webflow user id, name, email and settings. It is kept for 30 days in case you install again, and is deleted after that. Your Sites page shows the date. To delete it sooner, use Delete account.

What stays in Webflow, and how to remove it

AI SEO Deck adds no code and no element to your site, so there is nothing to clean up on your pages. What you applied stays, because it is your content:

  • Titles and descriptions you applied are in the page settings. Change them there: in the Designer, open the page's settings and edit the SEO title and meta description.
  • CMS drafts are draft items in the collection you chose. Open the collection in the CMS and delete the items you do not want. They were never published by AI SEO Deck.

Delete your account

Account, Delete account cancels a paid plan at the end of its period and deletes everything AI SEO Deck stored: your sites, keywords, answers, Site health results, settings and the Webflow access tokens. The app stays listed in Webflow until you remove it there, as described above.
