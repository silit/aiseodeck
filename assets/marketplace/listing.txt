APP DETAIL DESCRIPTION
**AI SEO Deck shows whether AI engines recommend you for the searches that matter, then fixes your Webflow site where they skip you.**

People now ask ChatGPT, Google and other AI engines which product, studio or service to choose. AI SEO Deck asks them the way your customers do, for every keyword you track, and turns the answers into one score you can follow week by week. Then it shows what to change on which page, writes the fix from your own content and applies it to Webflow when you say so.

**What it checks**
- Six AI engines with live web search: ChatGPT, Google AI Overviews, Google AI Mode, Gemini, Claude and Grok
- Each keyword is asked as two real customer questions on the chat engines, while Google AI Overviews and AI Mode get the search itself: 10 answers per keyword in every check
- Whether each answer names you, links to your site, or recommends a rival instead
- The sites the engines read and cite, and the searches they run before they answer
- Search volume for every keyword, keyword ideas for your site, and the keywords where Google's AI already cites you
- A site audit of your published pages: titles and descriptions, structured data and FAQ schema, Open Graph, alt text and thin content, plus llms.txt, your sitemap and whether robots.txt blocks AI crawlers

**What it fixes**
- A fix plan for every keyword: the page that should win it, a new title and description, and answers to the questions behind it as FAQ schema
- Every change shows the current value next to the new one, read live from Webflow
- One click applies the fixes to Webflow as unpublished changes. Nothing goes live until you publish
- Optional CMS drafts: FAQ items, or a new page for a keyword that has none yet, created as drafts

**Inside the Designer**
- Open AI SEO Deck from the Apps panel on any page of a connected site
- See the keyword that belongs to the page and which AI engines name you for it
- Write a new title and description and apply them to the page, or add FAQ schema written from the page itself

**Reports**
- Every keyword is checked again on a schedule, with an email after each check
- A client report link you can share, white label on the Studio and Agency plans

**Good to know**
- The app works in English. Keywords can be checked in 11 languages and for 18 countries
- Webflow stays the source of truth: the app writes only when you press Apply, writes to the primary locale and never publishes your site
- Webflow access tokens are encrypted at rest and deleted when you disconnect or delete your account
- The brands in the screenshots are made up

**Pricing**
- Free: 1 site, 3 keywords, ChatGPT, Google AI Overviews and Gemini, checked every month. Fixes can be written and copied
- Solo: $19 a month, 1 site, 10 keywords, all six engines checked every week, fixes applied to Webflow
- Studio: $49 a month, 5 sites, 30 keywords, white label reports
- Agency: $149 a month, 20 sites, 100 keywords, white label reports

Yearly plans include two months free. Every paid plan starts with a 7-day free trial. Payments through Polar, cancel any time.

AI SEO Deck is not affiliated with or endorsed by OpenAI, Google, Anthropic, xAI or Webflow.
