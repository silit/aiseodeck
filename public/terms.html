The service

AI SEO Deck measures how AI assistants answer for the keywords you choose and suggests changes to your Webflow site. AI answers change between runs, regions and people; the numbers show trends and are not a promise of any ranking, traffic or result. Search volumes are estimates from a third-party data provider.

Your account

You need a Webflow account and the right to connect the sites you add. Keep your sign in email secure. You are responsible for the keywords, questions, rivals and content you enter, and for reviewing every suggestion and draft before you apply or publish it.

Changes to your site

We write to Webflow only when you press Apply, and changes arrive as unpublished edits and CMS drafts. Review them before publishing. Structured data and answers must describe what is true and visible on your page.

Plans and payments

Paid plans renew monthly or yearly until cancelled and are billed by Polar, our merchant of record. Cancel any time in Settings; the plan stays active until the end of the paid period. Limits per plan, such as keywords and checks on demand, are shown on the pricing page.
