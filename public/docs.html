How AI SEO Deck works

AI SEO Deck checks whether AI assistants name and cite your brand, and helps you fix your Webflow site so they do it more often.

1. Connect Webflow

Press Connect Webflow and choose the sites or workspace to share. AI SEO Deck asks for these permissions:

PermissionWhy
authorized_user:readYour name and email, to create your account and send reports.
sites:readSite names and domains, so we know which website belongs to your brand.
pages:readPage titles, descriptions and existing structured data for the audit.
pages:writeOnly when you press Apply: saves a new title and description, or JSON-LD structured data, to that page.
cms:readLists your CMS collections, so you can pick where FAQ drafts go.
cms:writeOnly when you press Add to CMS as drafts: creates draft items. Drafts are never published by us.

Nothing is ever published for you. Every change arrives in Webflow as an unpublished edit; you publish when you are ready.

2. Set up your brand and questions

Enter your brand name as people say it, other names, your domains and up to five rivals. Then choose the questions your customers ask AI assistants. We suggest a first set written from your homepage, in the language you choose.

3. Checks

Each question is sent, exactly as written, to seven AI engines: ChatGPT (OpenAI), Google AI Overviews, Google AI Mode, Gemini (Google), Perplexity, Claude (Anthropic) and Grok (xAI). ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, Claude and Grok are asked through their official APIs with live web search on. Google AI Overviews and AI Mode are read from Google results for your country and language through DataForSEO, a search data provider; when Google shows no AI answer for a question, the grid shows a dash.

For each answer we record whether your brand is named, where it ranks among the brands you track, whether your website is cited as a source, which rivals are named, which websites are cited and what the AI searched for. The free plan checks five questions every month on ChatGPT, Google AI Overviews and Perplexity. Paid plans check every week on all seven engines, with two extra checks on demand each month.

What the answers tell you

  • Visibility over time: your score after every check, and how many answers name and cite you compared with the check before.
  • How AI describes you: whether the answers that name you sound positive, neutral or negative, the words they use for you, and claims worth checking, such as an old price.
  • Where rivals win: the questions where an AI names a rival and not you, widest gaps first.
  • Sources: the websites the AI cites, which of your own pages it cites, and the searches it ran to write its answer.

AI answers vary between runs and people. Read the grid as a trend across many questions and weeks, not as a promise about a single answer.

4. Site audit

The audit reads your published pages, robots.txt, llms.txt and sitemap.xml from your live domain and checks: titles and descriptions, H1 headings, JSON-LD structured data, questions answered without FAQ schema, social preview tags, thin content, image alt text, page language, AI crawlers blocked in robots.txt, and whether the homepage says who you are (Organization schema).

The audit identifies itself with the user agent AISEODeck/1.0 (+https://aiseodeck.silitlabs.com/docs#bot) and only reads the domains of the sites you connected.

5. Fixes

  • Title and description: edit or ask for an AI suggestion written from the page, then apply.
  • Structured data: we propose Organization and WebSite schema for the homepage (from the business details in Setup) and FAQPage schema where the page answers questions. Existing schema on the page is kept; only the same types are replaced.
  • FAQ to the CMS: questions and answers become draft items in the collection you pick.
  • robots.txt and llms.txt: we show the lines to allow blocked AI search crawlers and give you an llms.txt to upload in Site settings > SEO > LLMs.txt.

Applying fixes to Webflow is part of the paid plans. On the free plan you can copy every suggestion.

Reports

After every scheduled check you get an email with the score and what changed. Studio and Agency plans can share a read-only client report link with their agency name on it.

Disconnect and delete

Settings > Webflow connections > Disconnect revokes access and deletes the sites of that connection from AI SEO Deck. Settings > Delete account removes everything we stored. Changes you already applied to Webflow stay on your site.
