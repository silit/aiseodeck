The service

AI SEO Deck measures how AI assistants answer for the keywords you choose and suggests changes to the pages of your Webflow site. AI answers change between runs, regions and people; the numbers show trends and are not a promise of any ranking, traffic or result. Search volumes are estimates from a third-party data provider. Suggestions are written by AI from your own pages: review them before you use them.

Your account

You need a Webflow account and the right to authorize apps for the sites you connect. Keep your sign in email secure. You are responsible for the keywords, questions, rivals and content you enter, and for reviewing every suggestion and draft before you apply or publish it.

Changes to your site

AI SEO Deck writes to Webflow only when you press Apply: the SEO title and meta description of the page you chose, or draft items in the CMS collection you chose. Changes arrive as unpublished changes and CMS drafts. AI SEO Deck never publishes your site and never deletes anything in Webflow. What you applied stays in Webflow when you disconnect a site, uninstall the app, change plans or delete your account.

Plans and payments

Prices are in US dollars (USD): Solo USD 19 a month or USD 190 a year, Studio USD 49 a month or USD 490 a year, Agency USD 149 a month or USD 1,490 a year. The free plan costs nothing. Taxes may be added at checkout. Paid plans renew every month or every year until cancelled and are billed by Polar, our merchant of record. Cancel any time on your Account page; the plan stays active until the end of the paid period. The limits of each plan, such as keywords, sites and checks on demand, are on the pricing page and in the guide.

Paid plans start with a free trial of 7 days. Polar takes a payment method at the start of the trial and charges nothing during it. If you cancel before the trial ends, you are not charged and your account moves to the free plan when the trial ends; otherwise the plan starts and its price is charged when the trial ends. Each customer can have one trial across the products of Silit Labs: Polar declines a second trial for the same email address or payment method, also when the first one was for another of our products. The checkout then says so and shows the price of the plan before you pay.

When a paid plan or a trial ends, your account moves to the free plan. Nothing is deleted: keywords and sites beyond the free plan's limits wait and are not checked.

Ending the service

You can disconnect a site, uninstall the app in Webflow or delete your account at any time; the guide and the privacy policy say what each one deletes.
